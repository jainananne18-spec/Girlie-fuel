🍌 Banana oats shake

Your original recipe makes about 3 servings. Values are estimates; check your milk label for accuracy.

Whole batch

5 medium bananas + 1 cup rolled oats (about 80 g) + 2 cups Magnolia low-fat milk (480 mL).

~1,065 kcal

One of 3 servings

Blend the full batch, then divide evenly into three portions. No added sugar needed.

~355 kcal

Fiber estimate

About 21 g in the whole batch, or around 7 g per serving.

~7 g
