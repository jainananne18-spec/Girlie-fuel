GIRLIE FUEL — iPhone setup

IMPORTANT: iPhones cannot install Android APK files. This package is an installable-style web app (PWA). To add it to your iPhone Home Screen, it needs to be published to a secure HTTPS website first.

EASY SETUP (computer recommended):
1. Extract this ZIP file. Keep index.html, manifest.json, sw.js, icon-192.png and icon-512.png together.
2. Upload the extracted files to a static web host that provides HTTPS (for example, Netlify Drop or another static hosting service you trust). Follow that host's current upload instructions.
3. Open the resulting HTTPS website URL in Safari on your iPhone.
4. Tap Share (square with upward arrow) → Add to Home Screen.
5. Name it “Girlie Fuel” and tap Add.
6. Launch it from the new Home Screen icon. Your checked meals and settings are saved in that browser on that device.

WHAT IT DOES:
- Shows a daily Filipino meal plan and estimated calories.
- Lets you check off meals and see calories checked/remaining.
- Includes your banana-oats shake recipe.
- Tracks daily water, steps, vegetables/fruit and protein habits.
- Lets you change calorie and fiber targets.
- Stores check-offs locally on your device/browser; it does not send your data to a server.

NOTES:
- This package is not a native App Store app and does not include an APK (APK is for Android).
- Calorie and fiber counts are estimates, not medical advice.
- For offline use, open the app once while online after adding it to your Home Screen; service-worker support depends on the host/browser.
